# 2024 Autumn TA Training

Compared to 2023 Autumn and 2024 Spring, the 2024 Autumn training adopted a more relaxed "lunch-meeting" format (reserving a meeting room in the department building, with easy-going discussion around a theme). This format was reused in later semesters. Many sessions reuse previous materials, but updated with the current semester's discussion questions.

## Syllabus (9 sessions)

1. [TA Panel — common traps and efficiency tips for new TAs](ta_panel.md)
2. [How to teach students debugging](debugging.md)
3. [How to do office hours and tutoring well](tutoring.md)
4. [Teacher exchange with Prof. Deng Junhui](teacher_exchange.md)
5. [Innovative design of programming assignments](pa_design.md)
6. [CS education and games](games.md)
7. [K-12 programming education](k12.md)
8. [Sharing by the undergraduate course-evaluation committee](course_eval_committee.md)
9. [Assignment grading and code plagiarism detection](grading.md)
