# Excellent Courses and Assignments

A collection of excellent courses and assignments (domestic and international) that are worth TAs' consideration.

[Princeton COS226 Data Structures and Algorithms](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/spring22/cos226/assignments.php) — assignments span both practical and broad domains.

[Tsinghua Computer Networks](https://lab.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/router/doc/) — the "build a router" lab has been refined over many years, along with its documentation and framework.

[CMU 15-292 History of Computing](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~15292/index.html) — a historical perspective that may inspire teaching.

[CMU 15-213 Introduction to Computer Systems](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~213/labs.html) — excellent programming lab design, with well-developed recitations and office hours.

[Nanjing University Computer Systems Fundamentals PA](https://nju-projectn.github.io/ics-pa-gitbook/) — a remarkable programming assignment design.

[UC Berkeley CS61B Data Structures & Algorithms HW0](https://inst.eecs.berkeley.edu/~cs61b/sp22/materials/hw/hw0/index.html#b-respectful-communication) — a carefully designed "assignment zero".

[Stanford CS107e Raspberry-Pi-based Computer Systems lab3](https://cs107e.github.io/labs/lab3/) — teaching debugging/testing through assignments.

[Tsinghua OS rCore](https://rcore-os.cn/rCore-Tutorial-Book-v3/index.html) — learn/teach operating systems in Rust.

[Shanghai Jiao Tong University OS](https://ipads.se.sjtu.edu.cn/courses/os/) — a great textbook by Prof. Chen Haibo.
